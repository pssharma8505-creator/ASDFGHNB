Industrial Electrical Panels & Solutions

We engineer high power drives and panels perfectly suited to continuous hot and heavy metallurgy applications.

1. Digital DC Drives & Panels

Using high performance microcontrollers and robust digital DC drives of Siemens, ABB, and Parker.

  • Load shear controls using multiple combined power units for multiple coupled motors.
  • DC drive panels up to 1200 kW utilizing 12-pulse or 6-pulse electrical systems.
  • DC drive field controller integration for high field currents (up to 200 Amps).
  • Closed-loop center-winders and precise tension control.

2. Digital AC Drives & VFD Panels

Reliable on-site solutions using premium digital variable frequency AC drives of Siemens, ABB, Danfoss, and Control Techniques.

  • Up to 1250 kW AC drive systems in rolling mills for inter-stand/roughing duties.
  • Up to 750 kW AC drive systems for demanding hot strip mills.
  • High efficiency dynamic vector controller modules to maximize energy savings.

The basic Variac is designed for stepless control of AC voltages and is ideal for laboratory test fixtures. It takes 230V AC input and delivers output variable from 0 to 270V or 300V (Single Phase) or 0 to 470V (Three Phase star arrangements), available with manual or motor-stepper drivetrains.
